Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Health Dashboard ("the App") is a personal health tracking application that syncs data from Apple Health to your own self-hosted server infrastructure. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is handled, and your rights.

Data We Access

The App reads health and fitness data from Apple Health on your device, including but not limited to:

  • Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, and VO2 max
  • Step count, distance, flights climbed, and active energy
  • Body weight, body fat percentage, and lean body mass
  • Sleep analysis and respiratory rate
  • Blood pressure, blood glucose, and oxygen saturation
  • Nutrition data (calories, macronutrients, micronutrients)
  • Workout sessions and exercise minutes

The App does not write any data to Apple Health.

How Your Data Is Stored

All health data is transmitted to a server that you deploy and control on your own AWS account. The App does not send your data to any servers operated by the developer or any third party. You are solely responsible for the security and access controls of your own infrastructure.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage data.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or tracking technologies.
  • We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third party.
  • We do not have access to your health data or your server.

On-Device Storage

The App stores sync state (anchors and timestamps) in local preferences on your device to enable incremental syncing. Authentication tokens are stored securely in the iOS Keychain. No health data is permanently stored on your device by the App beyond what Apple Health itself retains.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only network requests the App makes are to the server URL you configure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored on your own infrastructure, you have full control over retention and deletion. Signing out of the App removes stored authentication tokens from your device. Deleting the App removes all locally stored preferences and cached data.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
